Three steps and you're tuning

  1. 1

    Download

    Get the zip for Windows from the releases page and extract it.

  2. 2

    Drop it in

    Move FreeTune.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

  3. 3

    Sing

    Rescan plugins in your DAW (FL Studio, Ableton, Reaper and so on) and load it on a dry vocal track.
